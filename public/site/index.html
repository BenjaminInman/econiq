The framework

Four phases. Three disciplines.

Acquisitions, operations, and dispositions each change with the phase. Most operators run one playbook through all four.

The Cheetah

Phase 01 — Recovery

Contrarian Irrational desperation

Occupancy tightens off the bottom while rents stay flat. Construction starts are near zero. Sentiment is still negative — which is precisely why basis is available here and nowhere else.

Acquisitions

Buy. This is the phase where basis is set, and basis is the only variable you can never fix later.

Operations

Chase occupancy over rate. Staff ahead of the curve so you're not hiring into a tight labor market later.

Dispositions

Hold. Selling here converts a timing advantage into someone else's.

Who built this

Most operators can underwrite a deal. Almost none can name the phase they’re buying into.

I've acquired over $500 million in multifamily and operated 4,500 units across two full cycles — the first from inside the business, the second with my own capital in the deals. Questions

Before you enroll.

How new is this framework?

Built late 2025 through mid 2026. It came out of twenty years of operating and a set of specific, costly mistakes — not from a track record it produced. That's why every call gets published and dated rather than asserted.

Is this for me if I haven't done a deal yet?

Yes, and arguably more so. The expensive mistakes happen on the first few deals, and phase awareness is cheaper to learn before you have capital committed than after.

What's the difference between Econiq Method and Cignal System?

Econiq Method is the training — how to read a cycle. Cignal System is the software that runs the read against live market data. Learn it at Econiq, apply it with Cignal.

Read or watch?

Recon launches as a read-through with embedded assessments. The video version follows, and enrollment includes both when it ships.

Does this predict market timing?

No. The sequence of indicators is reliable; the timing and severity are not. This teaches you to read the ordering earlier than operators using trailing data — not to name a date.
