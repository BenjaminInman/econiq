What Part One Covers

Part One applies the Rule of 6 to the complete acquisition lifecycle. Not just the purchase—the entire ownership journey that follows it: the hold, the operation, the exit, and the redeployment of the equity into whatever comes next.

Every lesson asks the same question from a different angle: given where the cycle sits right now, what is the right decision? Who should be on your team for this phase? What asset class offers the best risk-adjusted opportunity? When does the signal evidence justify acting? Where in the market does the thesis hold? Why does this deal fit your strategy at all? And how should the acquisition be executed so that it survives the phase that follows?

The answers change with the phase. That is the entire point. A partner, an asset class, a location, or a capital structure that is right in Recovery can be exactly wrong in Oversupply—and the investors who struggle most are rarely the ones who chose badly. They are the ones who did not change their choices when the cycle changed.

How Part One Builds on Special Ops

In Special Ops, you learned to read the cycle. You built the three-layer Onion Framework, learned to separate leading from trailing indicators, designed your Cignal Dashboard, and set the Cignal Threshold that tells you when accumulated evidence justifies action. Part One is where that intelligence becomes acquisition decisions.

Your dashboard answers the WHEN. The Onion Framework, applied at the submarket level, answers much of the WHERE. The discipline of leading indicators is the foundation of the HOW. What Part One adds is the rest of the decision—the partners, the asset class, the thesis—and the discipline of answering all six questions together, for the phase in front of you.

How to Work Through Part One

  • Read the lessons in order. The Rule of 6 begins with WHO for a reason: every other decision is only as strong as the team that executes it. Lesson 3.1.7 depends on everything before it.
  • Apply each lesson to a real deal. Use an acquisition you have completed or one you are pursuing. The frameworks are most useful when they are tested against a decision that actually cost or earned you money.
  • Complete each Knowledge Check before moving on. Each one ends with a scenario that asks you to apply the lesson, not recall it.
  • Keep your tools beside you. Your Cignal Dashboard and Market Maps on the Cignal System turn the lessons from principles into a working process.

What You Will Be Able to Do

By the end of Part One, you will be able to evaluate any acquisition through all six questions of the Rule of 6, calibrated to the phase; recognize when a strategy that worked in the last phase has stopped fitting the current one; and plan the full rotation of your equity across a cycle rather than one deal at a time.

The Rule of 6 decides whether a deal is right. 2XX2 decides what happens to the equity after the deal has done its job. Part One teaches both.
