Overview

Same disciplines. Different phase. Different answer.

Every program in this industry teaches acquisitions, operations, and dispositions as if the market were a constant. It isn't. The right rent strategy in expansion is the wrong one in hyper supply. The acquisition that builds a fortune in recovery destroys equity at the top.

Recon teaches you to establish where you are first — then gives you the playbook for that position across all three disciplines.

What you'll be able to do

Name the phase your submarket is in, and defend it
Separate leading indicators from trailing confirmation
Spot correlation being sold as causation
Adjust underwriting to phase rather than to sentiment
Set rent and renewal strategy by cycle position
Recognize a disposition window before comps confirm it
Price the cost of a tax or lender deadline against your read
Build a repeatable indicator dashboard for your markets

FAQ

Questions.

Will this tell me when the market is going to turn?

No. The sequence of indicators is reliable and documented back through the 1800s. The timing and severity are not. This teaches you to read the ordering earlier than operators watching trailing data — not to name a date. Anyone promising a date is selling something.

Has this framework produced a track record?

Not yet. It was built late 2025 through mid 2026, out of the mistakes described above — it did not create the portfolio, the portfolio is what taught me it was missing. Every phase read I publish is dated and stays up so the record builds in public.

I haven't done a deal yet. Is this too advanced?

No, and arguably you're the ideal student. The expensive mistakes happen on the first few deals, and phase awareness costs nothing to learn before capital is committed.

How long does it take?

About five and a half hours of material. Most people run it over two weeks so the submarket exercises have time to sink in. It's self-paced and access doesn't expire.

What's the difference between this and Special Ops?

Recon is the framework. Special Ops is applying it live, in a cohort, against your actual pipeline — including the full Collision case study with dates and numbers.

Do I need Cignal System?

No. Recon teaches the read manually, which is how you should learn it. Cignal automates it once you know what you're looking at.
