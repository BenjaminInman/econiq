The Recon Workbook

Synthesis, Application, and the Capstone Thesis

“The work that produces the operator. Take it seriously, and the operator you become will reflect the seriousness of the work.”

Welcome to the Workbook

You have completed the six modules of Recon Tier 1. You have absorbed new analytical vocabulary, examined the four phases of the multifamily cycle, studied the historical patterns that recur across decades, understood the economic forces that drive cyclical behavior, learned to recognize phase transitions in their early stages, and produced your Personal Bridge Document mapping your prior education across the cycle lens.

This Workbook is the culminating instrument of Recon. It does work that no individual module Knowledge Check could do: it tests your ability to integrate concepts across the full curriculum, apply cycle-aware thinking to realistic multifamily scenarios, and produce a capstone thesis that demonstrates the synthesis capability cycle-aware operators need.

The Workbook is also the second substantial artifact you will produce in Recon. Your Personal Bridge Document captures your own multifamily methodology integrated through the cycle lens. The completed Workbook demonstrates your synthesis capability applied to scenarios that approximate real multifamily decisions. Together, these two artifacts serve as visible evidence—in your own writing—that the methodology has produced operational capability.

How to Engage With the Workbook

The Workbook requires 4–8 hours of focused work, ideally completed across two or three sessions rather than in a single sitting. Engagement principles that will produce the strongest outcomes:

  • Complete sections in order. The Workbook builds from foundation synthesis through phase recognition through practice area application to the capstone scenario. Earlier sections develop the analytical muscle that later sections require.
  • Write substantively, not minimally. The answer spaces are sized for genuine analytical engagement. Brief one-line responses produce thin Workbook completions that don’t reflect synthesis capability. Operators who treat the Workbook seriously produce documents that become reference material for years.
  • Reference back to module materials. The Workbook tests integration, not memorization. If you find yourself uncertain about a concept while completing an exercise, return to the relevant module material rather than guessing.
  • Engage with the capstone scenario seriously. Part 4—the Capstone Scenario—produces the most substantial deliverable in the Workbook. Plan to spend 1.5–2.5 hours on this section alone.

    • The Workbook does not have a passing score in the conventional sense. It produces a deliverable that demonstrates your synthesis capability. Operators who engage seriously produce strong deliverables. The Workbook reflects what you put into it.

    This is the proof artifact. The depth of your engagement determines what it proves.

    Background

    You are the principal of a multifamily sponsorship firm with 4,500 units under management across five Sunbelt metros. Your strategy has historically been value-add renovation of mid-1990s and 2000s vintage product. You have $40 million of dry powder available for deployment, an established LP base of 65 active investors, banking relationships with three primary lenders, and operational infrastructure capable of integrating 1,500–2,000 additional units over a 24-month timeline. Your team consists of a chief investment officer, a chief operating officer, two acquisitions associates, and an asset management group of four professionals.

    Current Market Conditions

    Macroeconomic layer: Federal Reserve has held rates steady for three quarters after a sustained tightening cycle. The 10-Year Treasury yield has stabilized at elevated levels. The yield curve remains modestly inverted but has flattened from peak inversion. Headline CPI has moderated toward target. GDP growth has slowed to roughly 1.5% annualized. National multifamily layer: Transaction volume has declined approximately 50% from peak levels. National multifamily building permits are down 35% from peak. Capital flows into multifamily have moderated significantly. Lender posture has tightened meaningfully. National multifamily fundamentals are mixed—rent growth has decelerated to low single digits, vacancy has risen modestly, concessions have expanded. Submarket layer: Significant new supply is delivering from prior pipeline decisions; concession activity has expanded substantially; days on market have lengthened; absorption has slowed; rent growth has decelerated and turned modestly negative in two of your five markets.

    Strategic Question

    Your acquisition committee meets in 30 days. Your LP base is asking what your strategy will be for the next 12–24 months. Your operational team needs guidance on portfolio-level priorities. Your banking relationships are inquiring about your capital deployment plans. The cycle-aware investment thesis you develop will inform every strategic decision your firm makes for the next 24 months.
